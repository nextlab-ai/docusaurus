# What is netMeter C Series?

netMeter C Series is a portable, AI-powered diagnostic tool for pinpointing Ethernet cable faults using advanced TDR analysis.

## Consistency and easy to use

Optimized for field use, it interprets signal reflections to identify disconnections, shorts, and anomalies. Its intuitive interface and automated analysis reduce interpretation errors, supporting fast and reliable troubleshooting. By standardizing field workflows across teams, the C1 streamlines on-site operations and simplifies training.

## AI Automation

AI automatically analyzes physical defects, such as minor damage to cables hidden inside walls and corrosion of connectors, to pinpoint the exact location and cause of the problem. This shifts the reliance on engineers' intuition and experience to a data-driven, scientific diagnosis, dramatically increasing the "first-visit resolution rate."
